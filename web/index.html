HOW IT WORKS

Ask once. TILIK does the legwork.

GLOSSARY

Robinhood Chain & DeFi, in plain words

Quick definitions — tap "Ask" for a fuller explanation with live examples.

Robinhood Chain
A blockchain (chain ID 4663) for tokenized assets and apps, compatible with Ethereum tools.
Tokenized stock
A token that tracks the price of a real-world share, so it can trade on-chain.
Liquidity pool
Two tokens locked in a smart contract so people can swap between them.
Liquidity
How much value sits in a pool. Low liquidity means big price moves on small trades.
24h volume
The value traded in the last 24 hours. Huge volume on tiny liquidity is a red flag.
FDV
Fully diluted valuation: price × the maximum token supply.
Contract address
A token's unique ID. Symbols can be copied — always check the address.
Gas
The fee paid to have a transaction processed on the chain.
Block
A batch of transactions added to the chain; block numbers only go up.
Stablecoin
A token designed to stay at a fixed price, usually $1 — USDG is one.

FAQ

Good to know

Short answers to what people ask first. Anything else — just ask TILIK.

Read the docs
Where do the answers come from?

Live facts — prices, transactions, news, repository contents — come only from the sources TILIK retrieves for your question, and every one is numbered and linked. If something can't be retrieved, TILIK says so instead of guessing.

Which AI models answer?

OpenRouter is the primary provider, with Gemini and Groq as automatic fallbacks. Each model is tried at most once per question, so a slow or failing provider never stalls your answer for long.

Is my chat history stored on a server?

No. Conversations are saved only in this browser. Delete a chat from the history menu and it's gone from this device. Only the recent turns of the chat you're in are sent along with a follow-up question.

Can I paste any link?

Public web pages and GitHub repositories, yes. Private networks, local addresses and unsafe links are blocked before anything is fetched.

Is this financial advice?

No. Market and on-chain data are shown as facts with their sources and time; analysis is labelled as analysis. Always do your own research.
